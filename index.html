AboutBudapest · Hungary

Building images that feel both true and composed — whether they start in a viewfinder or a render farm.

Benjamin Fehér is a director and director of photography working across animation and live action. On Stairs, a five-minute allegorical animated short, he directed and led cinematography and look development — carrying a filmic, photographic sensibility into a fully constructed image.

His work moves between long-form patience and short-form precision: the same eye, different clocks.
